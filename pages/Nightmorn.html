Nightmorn was a multiplayer cooperative 2d top down horror game where you fight intelligent super natural monsters using a range of equipment.
Nightmorn was the third year group project I worked on with a team of 10 other people, including 3 other programmers.
We continued to develop the project for roughly a year after university in an graduate scheme called incubator, which ran out of funding in March and we opted to cease working on the project after that.
During the projects course I served as lead programmer and technical director of the company we formed post university until I had to stepdown to health issues in Feburary.
The game itself is actually quite fun and the demos are still avaliable on itch.io (linked above)

For this project I serve as lead programmer and netcode specialise and systems arcitect - but most of my work is UI programming with Unity UI-Toolkit.

I designed a lot of the main overarching such as the equipment system, the basic entity system that monsters and players inherit from, level life-cycling and loading, the audio system and the view cone system.

The other programmers specialisied in the general AI arcitecture for the monsters the implementation and tuning of these monsters, and the complicated player animation system.

The View System

Nightmorn has a viewcone to restrict each player's view to make the game feel more isolating. The effect has a small circle around the player, and a cone where they are facing.

The area out side the cone is desaturated, while monsters arent visible at all.

Theory

To create the view cone, a mesh is generated by raycasting against the scene so environmental objectises like walls and trees can block the cone. The edges of the cone need to blurr to create a smooth transtion. Monsters need to only appear inside the view cone and be hidden outside it.
